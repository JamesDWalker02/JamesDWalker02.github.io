My motivation

Why I'm fundraising

My mum lost both of her parents to cancer. She lost her mum while most of her siblings were still teenagers, and her father when I was a baby. I never got to know my grandparents, and I want to raise money in their memory.

I have also lost other members of my extended family to cancer, while fortunately seeing people close to me survive it. Experiencing both sides has shown me not only the devastating impact cancer can have on individuals and families, but also the difference that treatment and continued research can make.

I'm starting with a £750 target for Cancer Research UK in memory of family members and friends I have lost, and in support of everyone affected by cancer. I hope that raising money for research can contribute towards improving outcomes for others in the future.
